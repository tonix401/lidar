Calibration

Finds where the sensors stand: first the empty scene (nobody in it), then one person walks a wide loop through the area every sensor sees, for about 30 s. Each sensor's floor gives its tilt and height, the walk its position and turn, and the walls seen by several sensors refine it to about a centimetre. Mount every sensor within 10° of level, seeing some floor.

SensorPoseQualityProblems

From above, every sensor's empty scene in the solved poses: walls seen by several sensors should lie on top of each other.

Walls or things outside the area you care about can be left out: draw its outline on the view (click the corners, click the first one to close, drag a corner to move it), then solve again inside it. Everything outside is thrown away.
