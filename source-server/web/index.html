Calibration

Finds where the sensors stand: first the empty scene (nobody in it), then one person walks a wide loop (at least 2 m across) through the area every sensor sees, for about 30 s. Every sensor must be mounted level. Each sensor's floor gives its height, the walk its position and turn (every pair of sensors that saw the walker together counts), and the walls seen by several sensors refine it to about a centimetre. A sensor that sees no floor gets its height from the walker; you can also type it in.

From above, every sensor's empty scene in the solved poses: walls seen by several sensors should lie on top of each other. To correct a sensor by hand, drag its dot (move) or the tip of its arrow (turn), or click it and use the arrow keys (1 cm; with Shift 10 cm) and [ ] (0.1°; with Shift 1°). The numbers in the table nudge the same way.

From the side (x across, height up): every sensor's floor should lie on the line at 0. Drag a sensor up or down to set its height.

Walls or things outside the area you care about can be left out: draw its outline on the view (click the corners, click the first one to close, drag a corner to move it), then solve again inside it. Everything outside is thrown away.
